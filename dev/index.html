Final Testing & In-Game Preview Instructions:

  1. In the pattern preview tool press Console (Copy Dev localStorage)
  2. Go to https://main.openfront.dev/
  3. Right click on the page and select "Inspect" / F12
  4. In the top bar of the Inspector click the "Console" tab
  5. Directly paste into the console ( after the>)
  6. Open a solo game and see how the pattern looks.

I recommend testing not just FFA but also Teams as the primary is replaced in teams and you want to ensure your secondary won't be confused with other team colors. Currently you can test 2 Teams to see Red and Quads to see Orange/Yellow. You have to use your own judgement for the other team colors.
TLDR; If your pattern uses a color for secondary and most of your pattern is that color it may be declined as it is confusing in team games.
